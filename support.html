Common questions

How do I pair with my partner?

On both phones, open Pair a new partner — one phone shows a QR code and the other scans it, in person or from a photo you send however you like. Scanning sends a request the other phone has to accept, and both phones then show a matching safety number you can read to each other to confirm the connection is genuine. Your keys are exchanged through the QR code itself — we never generate or hold them.

Do I need a second phone?

No. Creating, tuning and saving cues all work on one phone, on the free tier, with nothing to set up — just tap Create a scene. A partner and a second phone are only needed when you want to send a cue to someone.

I enrolled a new fingerprint and can't unlock with biometrics.

For your security, changing your device's fingerprints or face data resets biometric unlock. Sign in with your password, then re-enable it from Settings → Identity & security.

I subscribed but don't see Enhanced scenes.

Open Settings → Premium and tap Restore purchase, and make sure you're signed in to the same Google account that bought the subscription. The app re-checks your subscription each time it opens.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Google Play Store → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → The Cue → Cancel. You keep Premium until the end of the paid period.

How do I delete my data?

Everything The Cue stores — your profile, keys, messages and saved scenes — is on your phone, and uninstalling the app permanently erases all of it. A message in transit sits on our relay only until your partner's phone collects it, encrypted the whole time, and is deleted on delivery. We keep no readable copy of anything.
