Common questions

How do I pair with my partner?

Pairing is done in person. On both phones, open Pair a new partner — one phone shows a QR code and the other scans it, then swap. Your phones exchange keys directly, so no server is ever involved.

Just want to try it alone?

Tap Test with just this phone on the Home screen to explore scenes and messaging on a single device — no second phone needed.

I enrolled a new fingerprint and can't unlock with biometrics.

For your security, changing your device's fingerprints or face data resets biometric unlock. Just sign in with your password, then re-enable biometric unlock from the Home screen.

I subscribed but don't see Enhanced scenes.

Open About and tap Restore purchase, and make sure you're signed in to the same Google account that bought the subscription. The app re-checks your subscription each time it opens.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Google Play Store → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → The Cue → Cancel. You keep Premium until the end of the paid period.

How do I delete my data?

Everything The Cue stores is on your phone. Uninstalling the app permanently erases your profile, keys, messages, and saved scenes. We hold nothing on our servers.
