Privacy Policy

Effective July 18, 2026
The short version. The Cue is built to know as little about you as possible. Your profile, your keys, your messages, and your saved scenes never leave your phone — they're encrypted on the device itself. There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking, and no analytics. The only time any information leaves your phone is if you subscribe and turn on Enhanced scenes, which sends your scene selections to our AI provider to write that scene — and nothing about it is stored afterward.

1. What The Cue stores on your device

Everything The Cue needs lives locally on your phone, encrypted at rest:

  • Your display name and the password-derived keys that lock the app;
  • The cryptographic keys of partners you've paired with in person;
  • Your messages and your saved (favorite) scenes;
  • Your preferences and settings.

We never receive, upload, or back up any of this. If you uninstall the app, it's gone.

2. Messaging

Pairing happens in person by scanning each other's QR codes, so the two phones exchange keys directly. Messages are end-to-end encrypted and travel device-to-device over a local connection. They do not pass through, and are never stored on, any server we operate. We cannot read your messages, and neither can anyone else.

3. Enhanced scenes and Venice AI (optional, subscribers only)

The Cue can generate scenes in two ways. By default, and always on the free tier, scenes are generated entirely on your device — nothing is transmitted anywhere.

If you subscribe to The Cue Premium and enable Enhanced scenes, then each time you generate a scene your selections — the categories, preferences, any names you enter, anatomy selections, and any special request text — are sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to our AI provider, Venice AI, which writes the scene and returns it. This is processing by a service provider on our behalf. We do not store this information on any server, and it is used only to produce your scene. Venice processes the request under its own privacy policy. If you prefer that nothing ever leaves your phone, keep Enhanced scenes off and use on-device generation.

4. Subscriptions and payments

The Cue Premium is an auto-renewing subscription sold through Google Play. All payment is handled by Google — we never receive or store your payment details. We only learn, through Google Play's billing system, whether your subscription is currently active so we can unlock Enhanced scenes.

5. No ads, no tracking, no accounts

The Cue contains no advertising, no advertising identifier, no third-party analytics, and no behavioral tracking of any kind. You do not create an account, and you never sign in to a service of ours — your in-app password is a local key, not an account.

6. Children

The Cue is intended only for consenting adults 18 years of age and older. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

7. Security

On-device data is encrypted (AES-GCM, with keys protected by the Android Keystore), the app can be locked with your password or biometrics, and its screens are marked secure so content doesn't appear in screenshots or the app switcher. No system is perfectly secure, but The Cue is designed so that even we cannot access your content.

8. Data retention and deletion

Because your data lives only on your device, you control it entirely. Messages can be set to auto-delete on a timer, saved scenes can be removed in the app, and uninstalling The Cue erases everything. We hold nothing on our side to delete.

9. Changes

If we update this policy, we'll revise the effective date above and post the new version here.
